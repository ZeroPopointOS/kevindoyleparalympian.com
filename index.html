The Operator

A system, not a story.

Kevin Doyle is Ireland's only SH2-classified precision rifle athlete, competing internationally on a defined pathway to LA 2028.

ZeroPoint is the system behind that pathway — five stages, engineered for one outcome: the same standard, repeated, under any pressure. The score is a byproduct. The process is the point.

Seven months after picking up the sport, Kevin took ZeroPoint to international competition for the first time. He came back with silver and a personal best. That's not a result — that's proof of concept.

Before sport, three decades in corporate IT leadership — standing up infrastructure across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. Same discipline, same systems thinking, now applied to a different target.
