The Athlete

A precision athlete
operating at international level.

Kevin Doyle is an Irish Paralympic precision rifle athlete based in Ireland, competing internationally, and building a structured pathway to LA 2028.

Kevin competes in SH2, the Paralympic rifle classification for athletes whose upper-limb impairment requires a shooting stand to support the rifle. He is Ireland's sole competitor in this classification.

ZeroPoint is a performance system Kevin built and operates himself — a defined methodology for repeatable execution under pressure. In October 2025, seven months after picking up the sport, he took it to international competition for the first time. He came back with silver and a personal best score.

That is not a result. That is proof of concept.

Before sport, Kevin spent three decades in corporate IT leadership — standing up infrastructure for multinational greenfield sites entering Ireland, then managing IT operations across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. That background informs how this operation is run, with the same discipline, systems thinking, and accountability that high-performance organisations demand.

The Process

I don't train for scores.

Every shot follows a defined process — controlled, repeatable, and refined over time. This is not about eliminating movement or chasing perfect conditions.

It is about executing under pressure, consistently and without compromise. The score is a byproduct. The process is the point.
