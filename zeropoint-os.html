The System

ZeroPoint OS

Five stages. Not a training log — the operating system behind every shot, and behind every repeatable standard under pressure.

"I was so far into the process, I had to be told I had finished."

There was no existing system built for this — no domestic peer, no established programme for an SH2 pathway to LA 2028. So the system had to be built.

ZeroPoint was built to solve one problem: performance that holds under pressure, on demand, without relying on how the day feels. It was built on the range, tested at international competition seven months after Kevin picked up the sport, and it applies with no material change to any environment where the standard has to be repeatable — a trading floor, an operations room, a boardroom under deadline.

01

Lock In The Outcome

The outcome is fixed before execution begins, and it's never adjusted midway. It's never softened if conditions get difficult. Once it's set, it doesn't move to accommodate how the moment feels.

This applies at every level. The long-range outcome — LA 2028, qualification, a podium — is fixed and doesn't shift. At the level of a single shot, what's locked in isn't a score. It's the standard of execution itself: the perfect process, defined before the shot is taken, never chased or renegotiated afterward.

In Sport The outcome — execution of the process without deviation — is locked in before the shot. The long-range outcome, a podium in Los Angeles, is fixed and never renegotiated.
In Business The deliverable and the bar for it are agreed before the project starts. The outcome doesn't change to meet the bar, and the bar doesn't change to meet the outcome.

02

Lock The Process

The method never changes under pressure. The same sequence, the same checks, the same preparation — run identically whether it's a training session or a Paralympic qualifier.

Pressure creates the temptation to change something — grip tighter, rush the setup, skip a check that feels unnecessary in the moment. The process is designed precisely to remove that temptation as a live decision.

In Sport Every step of the shot process — whether it's shot one of a hundred in training or the final shot of a final — is the same.
In Business The process to deliver the locked-in outcome never changes. It runs the same every time — introduce variance, and you introduce different outcomes.

03

Control The Environment

Within a single shot, there is no shot before and no shot after — there is only this shot. Every element within it is controlled and executed the same way, every time, with no new steps introduced mid-process. While execution is taking place, there is no analysis — only execution. Review, analysis, and adjustment happen only between shots, never during one.

This isn't about eliminating pressure. Pressure at international level is a constant, not a variable to be removed. It's about eliminating everything else — making sure nothing beatable is left to chance, so the process holds up under the testing of international pressure.

In Sport Every element within the shot is executed the same way, every time. Nothing is analysed or changed mid-shot — only between shots.
In Business The agreed process runs without new steps introduced mid-delivery. Performance can't be analysed if new elements keep being added — critique happens during validation, before execution starts, not while it's running.

04

Feedback Loops

Every shot is measured and reviewed — not judged on feel, but on data. What the sight picture showed, what the shot group revealed, what changed between each shot.

Feedback stays on the data, not on emotion or external noise — crowd, heat, conditions. Only information relevant to improving the next shot is entertained. Training and practice are treated differently: training is where variables are tested and measured; practice is pure execution, with no analysis running alongside it.

In Sport SCATT trace data reviewed after every training session — hold, trigger timing, and stability tracked shot by shot. In practice, there is no analysis running — only execution.
In Business Feedback stays on what's needed to hit the deadline, not on who got it wrong. There's no hierarchy in the discussion — everyone is focused on the problem, not on blame.

05

100% Accountability

Full ownership of every result, always. No external explanation for a missed standard — wind, a bad night's sleep, a difficult range — is accepted as the final word on why a result fell short.

This is the stage that closes the loop. A missed score is reviewed only in terms of how to close the gap — never attributed to bad luck or conditions. The priority isn't refining what's already strong; it's raising what's falling short.

In Sport A missed score is reviewed only as a process gap to close — the priority is raising the weakest shots, not polishing the strongest ones.
In Business Missed targets are owned by the team responsible for the process, and the process is corrected — not the story around it.

The Result

A System Built To Repeat, Not To Peak.

ZeroPoint isn't a plan for a good day. It's built to produce the same standard on the days that don't feel good — which is the only standard that means anything under competition pressure, or under any pressure.
